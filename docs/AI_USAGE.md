# AI Usage Statement: Business Guide Website

## AI Systems Used

- **Netlify's AI website generator:** generated the first version of the site from my written requirements. 
- **Claude (Anthropic):** helped migrate the generated site into plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript

## How AI Contributed

I gathered the requirements for this site myself directly from the client, and I wrote out in detail everything the website needed to contain.

I gave those requirements to Netlify's AI generator, which produced a first version of the site. That version used a more advanced architecture that I wasn't familiar with, and it included some content that wasn't supposed to be there. I corrected my instructions and had it regenerated.

I then used Claude to migrate the generated site into HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, a simpler architecture that I understand and can maintain. The goal of the migration was to keep everything from the generated version intact, so that nothing was lost in the move.

After the migration, I made further changes by hand in the HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, including fixing links and adjusting formatting.

AI did not gather the requirements or decide what the site needed to contain. Those decisions were mine.

I also created the Logo, Company Profile and the Images that were chosen to be in the website, as well as the Domain and Webpage Hosting Setup.

## Review Process

I reviewed the generated site and corrected unwanted content before regenerating it. After the migration, I checked that everything from the generated version carried over, and I made my own manual fixes to the code. I read through AI-assisted output and edited anything that didn't match what the client needed.

## Responsibility

I take full responsibility for the final website, including its accuracy, quality, and appropriateness.
